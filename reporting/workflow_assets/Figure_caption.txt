SEPM workflow. Hyperparameters are tuned within the outer-training partition and frozen before training-only proportion selection. Each inner fold reconstructs preprocessing, the baseline model and SHAP ranking. After selection, ranking is recomputed on all outer-training data, and the final model is fitted on selected observations before evaluating all outer-test observations. Standard GridSearchCV F1 uses native class predictions; proportion-selection and outer-test F1 use a fixed probability cutoff of 0.5. Candidate retention grid Q = {0.40, 0.50, 0.55, 0.60, 0.65, 0.70, 0.75, 0.80, 0.85, 0.90, 0.95, 0.97, 0.98, 0.99, 1.00}. Exact validation-F1 ties favor larger proportions. d denotes the number of predictors.
